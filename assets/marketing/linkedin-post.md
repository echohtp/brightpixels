What if the most important pixels on your page could actually shine brighter?

I built BrightPixels to explore that.

It uses WebGPU to bring HDR highlights to web text and images—letting selected pixels go beyond ordinary reference white on compatible displays.

Wrap a text fragment or image. Adjust the intensity. When the HDR renderer is unavailable, the original content stays visible.

I'm interested in what this means for attention: a key detail, a reflection, or a moment that deserves emphasis.

The live demo is where you can experience the effect on a compatible HDR display:
https://echohtp.github.io/brightpixels/

Where would you use a little extra light in an interface?
